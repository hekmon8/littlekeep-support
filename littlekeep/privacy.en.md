# LittleKeep Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026. Applies to version 1.0.0, Bundle ID `com.hekmon.littlekeep`. Support and privacy contact: [huhh1998@icloud.com](mailto:huhh1998@icloud.com) · [Support](support.en.md).

## Photos and text on your device

LittleKeep is an offline photo organizing and memory-recording tool. It requests Photos permission only when you choose to use photo-library features, to read the photos you allow, create memory pages, and carry out organization or deletion actions that you confirm. You can revoke permission or adjust the photos you allow in iOS Settings.

Pages, selected photo copies, text, and settings are stored in the app's device storage. The current version has no account, advertising, developer analytics, cloud AI, or upload feature to developer servers. The developer does not collect your photos, text, contacts, location, or device identifiers from the app, and does not share this content with advertisers.

## Apple services and voluntary sharing

If you enable iCloud Photos, the system may download original photos from Apple and library deletions may sync to other devices; these actions are controlled by your Apple settings. iOS device backups are also controlled by your system settings. Apple's App Store, TestFlight, diagnostics, and any analytics you choose to share are governed by Apple's policies and your system choices.

Only when you initiate the action does the app export a page, image, PDF, or backup, or invoke the system share sheet. Exported backups are not encrypted and may contain text and photo copies; store them only in a trusted location. Services that receive shared files handle them under their own policies.

## Keeping, deleting, and withdrawing permission

Local pages remain until you remove them in the app or delete the app. Before deleting the app, back up data you want to retain; exported files and system backups must be deleted separately where they are stored. Deleting a page copy does not automatically delete the original in your system photo library.

Photo-library deletion requires confirmation. You can check Apple's Recently Deleted album for recovery options; photos are usually retained for 30 days, but may be permanently deleted earlier. Revoking Photos permission does not automatically erase page copies already saved. Reminders are optional local notifications that you can turn off in the app or in system settings.

## Support requests and policy updates

You can email [huhh1998@icloud.com](mailto:huhh1998@icloud.com) to contact the developer or make a privacy request; you do not need to file a public issue. Your email address, message body, and attachments you choose to provide are used to handle the request. Provide only what is necessary and do not send passwords or payment credentials. To request deletion of support material you voluntarily provided, contact the same email address. Email is handled by the email service, and the relevant platform processes it under its own policies.

If you contact the developer through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/hekmon8/littlekeep-support/issues), your submission is hosted by GitHub and public issues are visible to others. Provide only the app version, operating system, and steps needed to resolve the problem; do not submit private photos, backups, passwords, or payment credentials. You can edit or delete content you posted, or ask a maintainer in the same thread to handle it. GitHub's own data retention and platform processing are described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The current app does not send analytics events to the developer. If a future version adds optional analytics or changes data handling, this policy, in-app information, and App Store privacy labels will be updated together, and your consent will be obtained first where required.
