# LittleKeep Help and Support

LittleKeep helps you review photos, confirm organization actions, and keep selected photos and text in local memory pages. There is no subscription and no account is required. A released version will be a one-time App Store purchase; TestFlight is for test distribution and does not mean the app is publicly available on the App Store.

Issues are public. Do not upload private photo libraries, full backups, passwords, or payment information. This page does not promise a fixed response time.

## Frequently asked questions

- **I cannot see some photos.** Check Photos permission in iOS Settings. When you choose selected-photo access, the app can read only the photos you allowed. You can manage that selection in Settings.
- **Where did a deleted photo go?** After confirmation, Apple Photos performs the library deletion. Check the system's Recently Deleted album; if iCloud Photos is enabled, deletion may sync to other devices.
- **How do I save my work before uninstalling?** Export a memory backup from the app's settings. The backup contains text and photo copies and is not encrypted, so keep it in a trusted location; uninstalling may remove local app data.
- **How do I move to a new device?** Export a backup on the original device and import it on the new one. Importing merges pages rather than overwriting existing pages, and it does not alter the system photo library.
- **Purchases and refunds.** For a released version, purchase records, re-downloads, and refunds are managed by Apple. See [Apple's refund help](https://support.apple.com/118223). The developer does not receive your card or payment credentials.
