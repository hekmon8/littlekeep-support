# LittleKeep — Offline Photo Organizer & iPhone Photo Journal

English | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

LittleKeep is a calm, offline photo organizer and iPhone photo journal for making space in your library without turning it into an endless cleanup project. It guides you through a small set of photos, lets you keep, defer, or confirm deletion, and can turn selected photos and a few words into a private memory page on your device.

## A small, considered photo review

LittleKeep is designed for reflection, not bulk deletion. A session contains up to ten photos. For each one, choose **Keep**, **Delete**, or **Defer**. Before anything is removed, you review the choices and confirm again through Apple Photos. Keeping everything completes the review. Deferring everything records that you looked and leaves the decisions for another day.

After a review, you may choose a photo and add a short note to create a memory page. Pages can be searched by their text or category, edited one at a time, removed when you choose, exported to PDF, and included in a backup for moving to another device.

This is a photo declutter app for people who want a slower alternative to endless scrolling: no people recognition, aesthetic scoring, automated “best photo” judgments, or pressure to empty an entire camera roll.

## Privacy at a glance

LittleKeep works locally. It has no account, subscription, advertising, developer analytics, cloud AI, or developer-server upload feature. Your pages, selected photo copies, notes, and settings stay in the app's device storage. Photos are accessed only when you choose to use your library and can be restricted or revoked in iOS Settings.

Exports and backups happen only when you initiate them. Backups are **not encrypted** and may contain photo copies and text, so store them only in a location you trust. If you use iCloud Photos or device backups, Apple may process and synchronize content according to your Apple settings.

Read the complete [Privacy Policy](littlekeep/privacy.en.md).

## Frequently asked questions

### Why are some photos missing?

Check LittleKeep's Photos permission in iOS Settings. If you grant access to selected photos, the app can only see that selection. The app also intentionally excludes hidden content and shared-album streams; screenshots and system favorites are excluded by default.

### What happens when I delete a photo?

LittleKeep asks you to review the selection, then iOS asks for final confirmation before Apple Photos performs the deletion. You may be able to recover the photo from Recently Deleted, usually for 30 days. If iCloud Photos is enabled, the deletion may sync to your other devices.

### How do I keep my pages before uninstalling or moving devices?

Export a memory backup from the app's settings on the original device, then import it on the new device. Importing adds new pages; it does not overwrite existing pages or alter your system photo library. Because the backup is unencrypted, keep it in a trusted location.

### Does LittleKeep collect my photos or notes?

No. The current app does not send developer analytics events and does not upload your photos, notes, contacts, location, or device identifiers to the developer. See the [Privacy Policy](littlekeep/privacy.en.md) for details and exceptions involving Apple services and support requests.
